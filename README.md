# VayparSathi
AI-driven hyper-local business advisory and financial assistant for rural micro-entrepreneurs — developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH).

**VyaparSathi** is an AI-driven, hyper-local business advisory and financial assistance platform designed to support rural micro-entrepreneurs. The project aims to provide accessible business guidance, financial planning support, local market insights, and information about relevant government schemes through an easy-to-use digital platform.

This project was developed as part of the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)** for the problem statement: **“AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs.”**

### Key Objectives

* Provide AI-powered business advisory tailored to local conditions.
* Assist micro-entrepreneurs with financial planning and decision-making.
* Provide relevant government schemes and financial support information.
* Deliver hyper-local business and market insights.
* Create a simple and accessible interface for rural entrepreneurs.

### Technology

The frontend is being developed using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, with additional technologies and services to be integrated as the project evolves.
